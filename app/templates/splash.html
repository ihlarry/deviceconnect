Automated Clinical Auditing

The platform utilizes advanced **Google Gemini Pro AI** to automatically evaluate daily patient metrics against study protocol rules. The AI drafts precise clinical summaries, filters out sensor noise, and flags relapse alerts, letting medical researchers audit patient compliance in seconds.

Who & How We Help

Continuous recovery occurs in the days and weeks between clinic visits. Wide Lens Health helps researchers, clinicians, and patients collaborate to capture objective daily health insights.

Objective Patient Insights

Fitbit and Google Health trackers collect real-time physical activity, heart rate, and sleep metrics. By consolidating this data, we provide individuals with clear evidence of their physical recovery while empowering their medical team with daily, home-based health context.

Advanced Exertion & Survey Correlation

We correlate objective daily movement with subjective patient surveys (Pain metrics & PROMIS-10). This allows researchers to compare reported fatigue with actual physical data (resting HR, HRV, sleep efficiency) to immediately confirm physical flare-ups or identify self-reporting gaps.

Clinical Trial Interoperability

A unified data pipeline automatically moves patient-authorized wearable data into the Wide Lens cloud analytics layer. Researchers can easily merge clinical records with fitness metrics to support decentralized studies with minimal administrative overhead.

Early Relapse & Safety Alerts

The platform monitors critical cardiorespiratory markers. By identifying early relapse signals—such as a multi-day rise in resting heart rate combined with a sharp drop in HRV or sleep efficiency—we help clinicians intervene days before clinical symptoms escalate.

The Ingestion & Analytics Engine

A highly optimized, reliable, and secure infrastructure designed to handle messy real-world wearable data.

Approval-Gated Enrollment

HIPAA-compliant patient enrollment uses a secure, two-stage approval gate (Firestore `is_active`). Ingestion remains blocked until clinical researchers explicitly verify patient eligibility, minimizing API fees and protecting cohort boundaries.

Self-Healing Pipeline

Our high-frequency data ingestion engine employs a **3-day rolling overlap** that automatically corrects for wearable sync lag. Any late-syncing data points are self-healed, ensuring BigQuery data integrity without manual database intervention.

Intelligent Noise Filtering

We mathematically isolate hardware noise from actual heart rate. Using advanced window and cadence analysis, our system filters out false-high optical spikes caused by loose-fit slippage or mechanical **Cadence Lock** during active exercises.

Definable Care Windows

Research teams can configure customized care windows. Patient metrics are actively ingested and audited against study protocol rules (e.g. step guidelines and heart rate caps) for the exact duration of the study block.

Clinical-Grade Security

We prioritize user data privacy. Safe data storage, identity management, and pipeline flows are conducted through an entirely secure, cloud-hosted architecture compliant with clinical standards.

Cloud Analytics & SQL CTEs

Aggregated and intraday cardiorespiratory streams are automatically structured into high-performance BigQuery tables. Researchers can execute custom SQL analytics to explore cohort-wide physical patterns.
