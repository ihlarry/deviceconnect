# Feature Spotlight: AI-Driven Clinical Assessment
**Subtitle:** Moving from Data Overload to Clinical Intelligence in Pericarditis Research

## The Challenge: The "Data Avalanche"
In modern decentralized clinical trials, researchers are overwhelmed. A single participant wearing a Fitbit generates millions of heart rate, step, and sleep data points over the course of a study. For a medical researcher, manually reviewing this data to ensure protocol adherence is like trying to find a needle in a haystack.

## The Solution: The AI Study Assistant
Wide Lens Health has integrated the latest **Gemini Pro** artificial intelligence directly into the research workflow. Our AI-Powered Assessment engine doesn't just store data—it conducts a daily "clinical audit" for every participant, automatically.

---

## Core Benefits for Clinical Researchers

### 1. Automated Adherence Monitoring
The AI automatically calculates a daily **Adherence Percentage**. It checks steps and heart rate limits against your specific study protocols, instantly flagging "Red" or "Yellow" status for participants who exceed exertion limits.
*   **Benefit:** Zero-touch oversight. You only focus on the participants who need your attention.

### 2. Intelligent "Noise" Filtering
One of the biggest headaches in wearable research is "Sensor Noise"—false-high heart rate readings caused by a loose watch band or sudden wrist movement.
*   **Benefit:** Our AI uses high-resolution variation analysis to distinguish between a "Glitch" and a "Real Event." You can trust the data without second-guessing the hardware.

### 3. Early Warning Trends (The Relapse Signal)
In Pericarditis research, early detection is key. Our AI monitors the delicate relationship between **Resting Heart Rate** and **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**.
*   **Benefit:** The system can detect subtle physiological shifts that often precede a clinical relapse, giving you the chance to intervene days before the patient experiences chest pain.

### 4. Plain-Language Narrative Summaries
Forget looking at raw heart rate graphs. The AI provides a concise **Clinical Rationale** for every assessment.
*   **Benefit:** Get a high-level summary of a patient's entire week in seconds. The AI explains *why* it assigned a status, citing specific dates, peaks, and trends.
